Growth adds load to systems that were built for a smaller company. The weak points rarely announce themselves until the day they fail.

## What we do

1. **Find where it's fragile.** We map the operations the business depends on and where each one could break.
2. **Strengthen it.** We remove single points of failure, in systems and in people.
3. **Keep it running.** We build for the day something goes wrong, so the business keeps moving when it does.

## Questions founders ask

### What counts as an operation we can't afford to lose?

Any process where a stoppage would quickly reach revenue, customers or compliance.

### Is this only about technology?

No. Fragility often sits in a process that one person knows how to run. We look at systems and at people.

With a founder conversation and a deep audit. [See how we work](/how-we-work/), or read how we [transform what exists](/transform/).
