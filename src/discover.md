A business that works can still run out of road. The next source of growth is rarely sitting inside the current roadmap.

A company's future should not be limited by the way it operates today.

## What we look for

- **New products** built on what the business already knows and owns.
- **New markets** where the current offer travels with little change.
- **New revenue streams** that existing customers, data or capabilities make possible.

## From idea to working reality

Ideas are cheap. We take the ones worth pursuing and bring them to life: we build it, build it with your team, or guide your team through it.

Delivered in months, not years.

## How it starts

A founder conversation, then a deep audit of the business, then a proposal with a specific opportunity and its expected impact. [See how we work](/how-we-work/).

## Questions founders ask

### Do you only advise on ideas?

No. We bring them to life. Consultants hand you a report. We own the outcome with you.

### Who is this for?

Founders and CEOs of established companies, mainly in technology, who are looking for the next source of growth.

### What if the opportunity needs new technology?

That is where we are at home. AI changes every few months, so what we build is designed to adapt with it.
